🎯 Salesforce CTI Decision Flow

Step 1: What type of client is this?

👉 Small team (1–50 users)

  • Limited budget
  • Wants quick setup
  • No IT team
➡️ Go to Step 2A

👉 Mid-size (50–300 users)

  • Growing team
  • Needs reporting + automation
➡️ Go to Step 2B

👉 Enterprise (300+ users)

  • Multiple teams, regions
  • Needs AI, WFM, compliance
➡️ Go to Step 2C

Ask: Do you have developers?

⚡ Shortcut (Call Script)

  • Simple → Aircall / CloudTalk / Dialpad
  • Scalable → RingCentral / 8x8
  • Deep Salesforce → Vonage
  • Enterprise → Genesys / NICE / Five9
  • Custom → Twilio / Amazon Connect

🚫 Disqualification Triggers

  • No IT team → Avoid Twilio, Amazon Connect
  • Budget < $30 → Avoid Enterprise tools
  • Need in 1 week → Avoid Cisco, Avaya, Genesys
  • Only basic calling → Avoid NICE, Five9

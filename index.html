Step 1 — What type of client is this?

👉 Small Team (1–50 users)

  • Limited budget
  • Wants quick setup
  • No IT team
→ Go to Step 2A

👉 Mid-size (50–300 users)

  • Growing team
  • Needs reporting + automation
→ Go to Step 2B

👉 Enterprise (300+ users)

  • Multiple teams, regions
  • Needs AI, WFM, compliance
→ Go to Step 2C
